This request can't be expressed in Astrogator FQL with the pinned example KB. The package catalog has no EPEL entry, and there is no action for adding or enabling repositories.

You could add an EPEL (or `epel-release`) entry to the knowledge base, scoped to the RedHat side. The query would then be:
