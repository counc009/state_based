This request can't be expressed in Astrogator FQL with the pinned KB:

- **No service:** elasticsearch isn't in the catalog. The only services are ssh server, postfix, and apache server.
- **No package:** elasticsearch isn't a catalog package either, so it can't be installed.
- **No enable action:** services only support `start SERVICE service` and `stop SERVICE service`.

If elasticsearch were added to the KB, the closest query would be `start elasticsearch service`.
