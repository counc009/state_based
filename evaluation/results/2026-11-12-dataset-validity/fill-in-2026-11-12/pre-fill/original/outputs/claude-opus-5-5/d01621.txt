This request can't be expressed in Astrogator FQL with the pinned knowledge base:

- **metricbeat is not in the catalog.** The only supported services are ssh server, postfix, and apache server.
- **The language has no "enable" action for services.** Only `start SERVICE service` and `stop SERVICE service` exist.

To support this request, the knowledge base would need a metricbeat service entry (and a metricbeat package entry, if it also needs installing), and the language would need a service-enable action.
