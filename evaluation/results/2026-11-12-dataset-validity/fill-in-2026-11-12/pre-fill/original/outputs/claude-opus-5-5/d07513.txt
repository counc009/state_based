This request can't be expressed in Astrogator FQL with the pinned KB:

- **Archlinux is not a supported OS.** OS conditions accept only Debian, Ubuntu, RedHat, `Debian based`, and `RedHat based`.
- **ufw is not in the package catalog.** `installed` / `not installed` conditions only work with catalog packages: bash, zsh, postfix, apache server, ssh client, ssh server, and numpy.
- **There is no standalone "check" action.** Conditions can only guard actions inside `if ... then ...`.
