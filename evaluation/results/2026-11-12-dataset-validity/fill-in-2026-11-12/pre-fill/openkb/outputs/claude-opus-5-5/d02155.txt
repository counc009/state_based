This request can't be expressed in Astrogator FQL. The language has no action or condition for loading or checking modules, whether kernel modules or Apache modules.

The closest supported forms do something different:
- `install zeroconf` installs a package. It does not load a module.
- `if zeroconf running then ...` or `start zeroconf service` manages a service. It does not load a module.
