This request can't be expressed as a valid Astrogator FQL query:

- **Supervisor isn't in the knowledge-base catalog.** Only `ssh server`, `postfix`, and `apache server` are supported, so `start supervisor service` wouldn't resolve.
- **FQL has no action for starting a service on boot.** Only `start SERVICE service` and `stop SERVICE service` exist.

To support this, the KB would need a Supervisor service entry and an "enable at boot" action.
