This request can't be expressed in Astrogator FQL. Enabling cgroup memory and swapaccount means adding the kernel boot parameters `cgroup_enable=memory swapaccount=1`, regenerating the bootloader configuration (for example, `update-grub` or `grub2-mkconfig`), and rebooting. FQL has no action for kernel parameters, bootloader configuration, or arbitrary commands. Writing to `/etc/default/grub` and then running `reboot` would not apply the change without the regeneration step.
